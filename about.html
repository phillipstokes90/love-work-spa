Meet Lauren

My name is Lauren Copeland and I am a licensed cosmetologist. I have been doing nails and hair for eleven years now and I really enjoy doing it. I love doing hair and nails because of how my clients react and how it makes them feel.

My dream is to make every person feel pretty and be themselves. My salon is a safe place — a place to feel vulnerable and comfortable. I will do my best to achieve your look as much as I can and hope you enjoy it.

Thanks for choosing Love Work Spa to pour our love on you.
